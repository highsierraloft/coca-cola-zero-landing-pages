Rituals

Zero, your way.

The can is the starting point. Here are three ways the rest of us drink it.

01

Zero & Lime

The classic that makes any table feel like a terrace.

  • Fill a glass with cracked ice
  • Pour Zero to the brim
  • Give half a lime a hard squeeze
  • Stir once. Done.

02

The Midnight Float

Dessert that fizzes. Accept no substitutes.

  • Scoop vanilla ice cream into a tall glass
  • Pour Zero slowly down the side
  • Top with a pinch of sea salt
  • Eat fast, before it sings

03

Espresso & Zero

Coffee up, cola on top. The afternoon fix, fixed.

  • Pull a double espresso
  • Chill it over one big ice cube
  • Top with Zero Sugar
  • Best before 3 p.m. Probably.
