Straight answers

Zero fine print.

Does it really taste like original Coca-Cola?

That’s the whole brief. Zero Sugar is built to match the original’s taste profile — the caramel depth, the citrus top notes, the bite. Most people need a second sip to tell. Many never do.

Is it actually zero sugar, or just “low”?

Actually zero: 0 g of sugar and 0 calories per 330 ml can. Sweetness comes from a blend of aspartame and acesulfame K instead of sugar.

How much caffeine is in a can?

About 32 mg per 330 ml — roughly a third of a typical cup of coffee. Prefer none at all? The Caffeine-Free version keeps the taste and drops the caffeine.

What’s the difference between Zero Sugar and Diet?

Zero Sugar chases the taste of original Coca-Cola as closely as possible. Diet Coke is its own thing — a lighter, crisper taste with its own fan club. Both have zero sugar.

Where can I get it?

Anywhere drinks are sold cold: supermarkets, kiosks, vending machines, and that one fridge at the back of every good party.
