04 / GOOD QUESTIONS

A LITTLE
ZERO
KNOW-HOW.

Curious? Let’s clear a few things up.

Does Coca-Cola Zero Sugar really have no sugar?

Yes. Coca-Cola Zero Sugar contains no sugar. Sweeteners give it its sweet taste. Check the label on your can or bottle for the ingredients and nutrition information in your country.

Is Coca-Cola Zero Sugar the same as Diet Coke?

They both offer a sugar-free choice, but they have different recipes. Coca-Cola Zero Sugar is made to taste more like Coca-Cola Original Taste, while Diet Coke has its own distinct flavor.

Does Coca-Cola Zero Sugar contain caffeine?

Yes, the regular version contains caffeine. A caffeine-free version is also available in some countries. Look for “Zero Caffeine” on the pack and check your local Coca-Cola website for availability.

What’s the best way to enjoy it?

Chilled, with your favorite food and your favorite people. Pour over ice for an extra-refreshing sip. Store unopened cans in a cool, dry place and follow the guidance on the pack.

Where can I find my favorite Zero?

You’ll find Coca-Cola Zero Sugar in supermarkets, convenience stores, and restaurants. Explore places to shop. Flavors and pack sizes vary by country and retailer.
